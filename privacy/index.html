Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At Entry, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is processed, and how it is protected when you use our app and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to operate, maintain, and improve the app, which may include:

  • Account Authentication Data: Account identifiers provided during sign-in (such as email address and user ID) to manage account creation and authentication.
  • Journal Entries: Text entries submitted within the app along with associated timestamps for storage and cross-device synchronization.
  • Technical & Usage Data: Basic app diagnostic information, performance metrics, or error logs collected to maintain system stability and troubleshoot issues.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use collected data for purposes including:

  • Authenticating user access and managing account security.
  • Storing and synchronizing journal entries and user preferences.
  • Delivering scheduled push notifications or reminders.
  • Monitoring app performance, fixing bugs, and improving user experience.

3. Data Storage & Security

We employ commercially reasonable technical measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access or loss. While we work to protect your personal data, no security measure or transmission method over the internet is completely infallible.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your information as long as necessary to provide the service or satisfy legal obligations. You may request account or data deletion through the app settings or by contacting customer support.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our legal requirements or app operations. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

7. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries or data requests, please contact team@spekfinancial.com.
