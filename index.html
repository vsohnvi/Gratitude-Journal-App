Rule 01

150 Characters

Forcing you to distill your day into a single micro-thought removes blank-page anxiety and keeps daily entries under 30 seconds.

Rule 02

The Midnight Lockout

At 12:00 AM local time, the day seals permanently. You can't backfill yesterday or modify past reflections. Consistency is your only currency.

Rule 03

Smart Evening Nudge

Receive a gentle reminder at 9:00 PM (or your custom time). Once you log your entry, today's reminder automatically suppresses itself.
